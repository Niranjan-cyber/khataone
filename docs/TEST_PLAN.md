# Shopkeeper usability test: 15 minutes per shop

Use this when you can sit with a real kirana owner. It measures the brief's promise (credit entry in under 10 seconds) honestly.

## Before you start
- Phone: any Android, Chrome, open https://bahi-kirana.vercel.app (no login). Tap ⚙ → **Reset demo data** before each person.
- Ask permission. Say: "We are testing the app, not you. There are no wrong answers."
- Record: age band, years using a smartphone, preferred language, left or right hand.
- Do **not** show them how to use it first.

## Tasks (time each one from your "start" to the moment they finish)
| # | Say this (in their language) | Success when | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Suresh Patil ne ₹250 ka saamaan udhaar liya. Khate mein likhiye." | "Save ₹250 udhaar" tapped | seconds, taps, errors, needed help? |
| 2 | Same with Meena Joshi, ₹137. | saved | seconds, taps, errors |
| 3 | "A new customer, Deepak, takes ₹500 udhaar." | saved for Deepak | seconds, help? |
| 4 | "Oops, that was ₹50, not ₹500. Fix it." | Undo or cancel entry used | seconds, which method |
| 5 | "How much does Rafiq Shaikh owe you?" | says ₹2,140 | correct? |
| 6 | "Remind Suresh politely on WhatsApp." | WhatsApp opens with message | seconds, comment on tone |
| 7 | "Suresh pays ₹400 by UPI." (tell them to press Success, it is a demo) | partial payment recorded | correct balance read back? |

Repeat task 1 three times with different amounts and customers. **Report every attempt**, not just the fastest.

## Afterwards (2 minutes)
1. "Could you use this with one hand at the counter?" (yes / partly / no; why)
2. "Was anything confusing?"
3. "Would you say the reminder message to your customer?"

## Reporting
For task 1: median time, range, and the share of attempts under 10 s, plus taps and errors. Quote people only with their permission, and never invent quotes.
